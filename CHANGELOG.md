# Changelog

## v1.0.0 – 2026-10-30

First public release.

- Passes timed QTEs by itself: single presses, repeated presses and long presses, also with a random key.
- F3 toggle with on-screen messages; setting saved in `settings.txt`.
- One-click installer and uninstaller. Shares UE4SS with the other PBZ mods.
- Tested on Phantom Blade Zero `CL-301524_JKS-17219` (Steam) with UE4SS `v3.0.1-1185` experimental.
